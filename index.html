Arnold Schwarzenegger

Bodybuilder · Actor · Governor

Arnold Alois Schwarzenegger (born July 30, 1947) is an Austrian-American actor, bodybuilder, and former politician. He is widely regarded as the greatest bodybuilder of all time and a cultural icon of the 20th century.

He won the Mr. Olympia title seven times (1970–1975, 1980) and starred in blockbuster films such as The Terminator, Predator, Conan the Barbarian, and True Lies. From 2003 to 2011, he served as the 38th Governor of California.
